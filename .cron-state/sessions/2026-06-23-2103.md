## Shipped (5 commits)

1. **6427ac4** `feat(graph): --json --filter-completed-before/
   --filter-started-before/--filter-touched-before BEFORE-cutoff trio`
   Inverse-cutoff sister of the SINCE trio shipped in tick #30.
   Three flags, one architectural refactor: per-axis predicate
   evaluation with AND-within-axis and UNION-across-axes (matches
   the SINCE semantic). Two new envelope marker fields
   (filter_completed_before, filter_started_before), both
   omitempty. Use cases: stale-chain triage, long-running WIP
   alerts, fixed-width windows. 10 tests.

2. **ad4befb** `feat(graph): --json --append --max-bytes <B>
   caps JSONL file at byte size`
   Secondary file-size cap on the JSONL rotation path, layered
   atop --rotate. FIFO over whole records until under cap; last
   record always preserved. New helper rotateJSONLFileByBytes
   (sister of rotateJSONLFile). Composes with --rotate (both
   caps apply, stricter wins). 10 tests including direct unit-
   test for the helper.

3. **0ecc556** `feat(wip): --invert flips selector predicates
   (priority/tag/strict-and-tag)`
   Filter-modifier that flips selector-axis predicates from
   include-style to exclude-style. The --stale axis is NOT
   inverted (it's a quantitative window, not categorical).
   Selectors compose as AND across axes regardless of --invert
   mode. Filter-summary header surfaces inversion via '!'
   prefix per axis. Empty-result message also surfaces it.
   10 tests.

4. **887b569** `feat(log): --priority filters recent-completions
   by exact priority`
   First priority-axis filter on `tsk log`. The five filter-
   bearing verbs (wip, depend --pending, start --all, pause
   --all, log) now all share a uniform --priority surface.
   Composes with --since and --tag as AND. 8 tests.

5. **1037334** `feat(log): --strict-and-tag <CSV>
   intersection-style multi-tag filter`
   Completes the strict-and-tag axis symmetric coverage. The
   five filter-bearing verbs all expose the same CSV-
   intersection flag with identical semantics. Mutually
   exclusive with --tag. 8 tests.
